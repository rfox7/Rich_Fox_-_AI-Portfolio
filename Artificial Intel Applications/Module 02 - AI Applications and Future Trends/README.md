# Module 2 – AI and Its Applications & Future Trends in AI

## Key Concepts
- Rapid pace of AI advancement — new model releases and capability jumps happening quarter over quarter
- Open-source vs. proprietary models and how open-source is democratizing AI research and access
- Advances in NLP (multilingual/cross-lingual capability), computer vision, and robotics
- AI's expanding footprint across industries (healthcare, finance, manufacturing)
- Emerging technical directions: synthetic data generation, reinforcement learning, autonomous agents

## Tools & Technologies
- Meta's Llama 3 (open-source LLM)
- Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- OpenAI's o1 series and GPT-4o
- DeepMind's Gemini 1.5 Pro (multimodal)
- Mistral 7B (efficient open-source model)

## Real-World Examples
- Healthcare: diagnostics, personalized medicine, drug discovery
- Finance: fraud detection, algorithmic trading, risk management
- Manufacturing: automation, predictive maintenance, quality control

## Ethical Considerations
- AI regulation and policy (EU AI Act and other global frameworks)
- Bias, transparency, and accountability as AI adoption scales
- AI's growing role in shaping societal norms and public discourse

## Key Takeaway
AI capability is advancing faster than most regulatory frameworks can keep up with — open-source models are lowering the barrier to entry, which raises the stakes for getting governance and ethical guardrails right.
