# Module 4 – AI in Finance

## Key Concepts
- Core AI technologies used in finance: machine learning, NLP, and computer vision
- Algorithmic trading: high-frequency trading, pattern recognition, predictive market analytics
- Fraud detection: real-time transaction monitoring, anomaly detection, behavioral analysis
- Credit scoring: alternative data sources, automated risk assessment, faster loan approvals
- Portfolio management: robo-advisors, automated asset allocation and rebalancing
- Insurance: automated claims processing, risk-based premium calculation, personalized policy recommendations
- Regulatory compliance: automated reporting, real-time compliance monitoring, predictive compliance
- AI + blockchain: smart contracts, DeFi applications, AI-powered crypto trading

## Tools & Technologies
- Machine learning models for fraud/anomaly detection and credit risk scoring
- NLP-driven chatbots and virtual financial assistants
- Robo-advisor platforms for automated investment management
- Smart contracts and blockchain-based transaction security

## Real-World Examples
- **FinSecure Bank case study:** implemented ML-based real-time fraud scoring, cutting fraud losses by 60%, reducing the false-positive rate from 90% to 40%, and detecting new fraud patterns 80% faster
- HSBC fraud detection case study (see assignment below) — a second, real-world example of AI-driven fraud detection in banking

## Ethical Considerations
- Transparency: AI decisions must be explainable to customers and regulators
- Fairness: avoiding bias in models that could lead to discriminatory lending or pricing
- Data privacy in customer financial transactions
- Accountability: clearly defining responsibility when AI systems make financial decisions
- Regulatory compliance for responsible AI use in finance

## Key Takeaway
Finance is one of the most mature industries for applied AI — but also one of the most regulated, meaning every efficiency gain (fraud reduction, faster approvals) has to be balanced against explainability and fairness requirements that most other industries don't face yet.
