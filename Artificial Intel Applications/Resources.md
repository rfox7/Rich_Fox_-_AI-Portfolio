# Resources

A consolidated list of tools, platforms, models, and references cited across Modules 1–8 of ITAI 2372. Organized by module for traceability back to the source lecture. See each module's README for full context.

## Module 1 – Philosophy and AI & Ethical and Societal Implications of AI
- No specific AI tools/platforms — conceptual/philosophical module
- Reference case study: GDPR (AI governance and policy)

## Module 2 – AI and Its Applications & Future Trends in AI
- **Meta Llama 3** — open-source LLM
- **Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet**
- **OpenAI o1 series / GPT-4o**
- **Google DeepMind Gemini 1.5 Pro** — multimodal model
- **Mistral 7B** — efficient open-source model
- Reference: EU AI Act (regulatory framework)

## Module 3 – AI in Healthcare & AI in Agriculture
- Medical imaging ML models, clinical NLP, wearable/IoT health monitoring devices
- **Blue River Technology "See & Spray"** — precision herbicide application
- **Prospera** — AI-powered crop health monitoring
- Drones and hyperspectral sensors for crop/soil analysis
- Case study reference: Moderna (AI-accelerated mRNA vaccine design)

## Module 4 – AI in Finance
- Machine learning models for fraud/anomaly detection and credit risk scoring
- NLP-driven chatbots and virtual financial assistants
- Robo-advisor platforms for automated investment management
- Smart contracts and blockchain-based transaction security
- Case study references: FinSecure Bank (fraud scoring), HSBC (fraud detection)

## Module 5 – Smart Cities and Transportation
- IoT sensor networks and 5G/edge computing infrastructure
- Machine learning for predictive traffic analytics, occupancy prediction, pollution pattern recognition
- Computer vision for vehicle detection, pedestrian tracking, incident recognition
- V2X communication protocols (V2V, V2I, V2P)
- Digital twin platforms for city-scale simulation
- Mobility as a Service (MaaS) platforms
- City references: Stockholm, Ann Arbor (MI), Barcelona, Copenhagen, Milton Keynes, Hamburg, Singapore

## Module 6 – AI in Media and Entertainment
- **Music composition:** AIVA, Amper Music, Suno, UDIO
- **Art/design generation:** DeepArt, Artbreeder (GAN-based)
- **Video generation:** PikaLabs, Runway Gen-3, Meta MovieGen, Luma AI (Dream Machine)
- **Writing/NLG:** GPT-3 / GPT-4-class language models
- **Recommendation engines:** collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, hybrid models (Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime)

## Module 7 – AI and Blockchain Integration
- Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), Merkle tree cryptographic linking
- Consensus mechanisms: Proof of Work (Bitcoin), Proof of Stake, DPoS, PBFT
- **Ethereum** — smart contract platform
- Symmetric and asymmetric key cryptography
- Federated learning combined with blockchain for privacy-preserving AI training
- Case study references: Walmart (blockchain food safety + AI inventory), IPwe (AI + blockchain patent registry)

## Module 8 – AI in Retail
- Computer vision for shelf monitoring, self-checkout, loss prevention
- NLP-driven chatbots and voice shopping assistants
- Personalization/recommendation engines
- Warehouse robotics: picking robots, AGVs, automated sorting, inventory drones
- Smart store hardware: smart shelves, electronic shelf labels, interactive mirrors
