🔑 KEY THEMES ACROSS ALL LABS
=============================

1. THE REPRESENTATION PROBLEM
   How to encode data for downstream tasks. Evolution from raw pixels
   to hand-crafted features (HOG, LBP) to learned CNN features to
   multimodal embeddings.

2. GENERALIZATION VS. OVERFITTING
   Why training accuracy is misleading. Appears in every lab.
   Solution: Cross-validation, data augmentation, regularization.

3. LAB VS. REAL WORLD
   Lab metrics don't guarantee deployment. Bias, distribution shift,
   edge cases are the real challenges.

4. ETHICAL AI
   Confidence ≠ accuracy. Bias is systemic. Hallucinations happen.
   High-stakes decisions require human oversight.

5. ABSTRACTION & COMPOSITION
   Simple building blocks (tools) + clear interfaces (workflows) +
   persistent state (memory) = powerful, maintainable AI systems.
